Early Greek manuscripts of the New Testament

passages.json: for each passage on the site's Manuscripts pages, what each early Greek manuscript has there, its
own text of the passage and of the verses either side, what the printed editions print, and the English wording.

Built by tools/build_manuscripts.py from:
- Center for New Testament Restoration (CNTR), Electronic Transcriptions, by Alan Bunning.

  King James Version 1769 (Pure Cambridge Edition), World English Bible 2020, Berean Standard Bible 2023 (CC0).
