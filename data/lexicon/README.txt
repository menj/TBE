Strong's lexicon

g/{n}.json, h/{n}.json   Greek and Hebrew entries for Strong's numbers n*100 to n*100+99 ("G3056"): the senses
                         (word, transliteration, part of speech, gloss, definition), the number of times the
                         word is used in the interlinear, and up to 120 places.
g-index.json, h-index.json  every number with its word, gloss and count, sorted by the word.
metzger.json             Part I of Bruce M. Metzger, Lexical Aids for Students of New Testament Greek (new edition,
                         1969): the Greek words by how often they occur, with his meanings, matched to Strong's
                         numbers. Read by OCR from the scan; used by the owner's permission.

Built by tools/build_lexicon.py from STEPBible's Translators Brief lexicons of Extended Strongs, TBESG (Greek,
definitions after Abbott-Smith, Manual Greek Lexicon of the New Testament) and TBESH (Hebrew, definitions after the
abridged Brown-Driver-Briggs), https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data, CC BY 4.0, data created by
STEPBible.org; and by tools/build_metzger.py from https://thebible.exposed/pdf/lexical-aids.pdf.
